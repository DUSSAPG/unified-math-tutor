# Brand Positioning

## Public Brand

- Brand: Math Intelligence
- Hero: Develop mathematical thinking. Unlock your potential.
- Supporting statement: Personalised learning. Measurable progress.
- Technology positioning: Powered by Adaptive Learning Intelligence

## User Roles

- Learner role: I'm a Learner
- Supporting role: I'm supporting a learner
- Supporting-role scope: parents, guardians, teachers, tutors, homeschool educators and learning support staff

## Shared Reporting Language

- Shared reporting layer: Learning Analytics
- Supporting copy: Understand progress, identify learning gaps and support the next step.

## Ownership Component

- Ownership component: My Maths Journey
- Named variant: <First name>'s Maths Journey

## Accuracy Guardrails

- Adaptive Learning Intelligence is brand positioning.
- Do not claim real-time AI diagnosis, autonomous generation, guaranteed outcomes or cloud synchronisation unless those capabilities are implemented.
- Learning Analytics should display real local progress data or an honest empty state.
