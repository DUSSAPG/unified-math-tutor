## 1. Introduction

Sterling Math ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Sterling Math mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Parent email address** (optional, entered during onboarding): used only to send learning progress reports. Never used for marketing without explicit consent.
- **Name / learner nickname** (optional): used only to personalise the in-app greeting.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **App usage data** (locally stored only): practice session results, streak counts, topics completed. This data is stored on-device and is not transmitted to our servers in the current version.
- **Crash reports** (if analytics is enabled in a future release): anonymised diagnostic data to improve stability.

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect precise location data.
- We do not collect device contacts or media.
- We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID).
- We do not sell data to third parties.

## 4. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for use by children under parental supervision. We comply with:

- **GDPR** (EU/EEA) — Article 8: parental consent required for children under 16 (or the applicable national age).
- **UK GDPR** — Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code).
- **COPPA** (US) — we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
- **PIPA** (South Korea) — personal information of children under 14 requires guardian consent.

We collect the minimum data necessary. The parent email field is clearly marked optional.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- All on-device data is stored using platform-standard secure storage.
- If a parent email is collected, it is stored with industry-standard encryption.
- We do not transmit learner performance data to external servers in the current version.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

- On-device data is deleted when the app is uninstalled.
- To request deletion of any data we hold (e.g. a submitted parent email), contact us at **support@mathtutor.app**.
- We will action deletion requests within 30 days.

---

## 7. Third-Party Services

The current version of Sterling Math does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or payment SDKs. If third-party services are added in future versions, this policy will be updated and users will be notified.
