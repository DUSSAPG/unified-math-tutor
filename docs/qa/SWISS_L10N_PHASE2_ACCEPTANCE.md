## 6. Subject-matter decisions needed (stop-and-report items)

These terms could not be settled reliably without a subject-matter decision. No guess was written to the ARBs.

1. **D-1 Swiss Gymnasium (fr/it).** No draft was written. The school-type name varies by canton ("Gymnase", "Collège", "Lycée", "Liceo"). The pack is locked, so the label is not shown today.
2. **D-2 `helpTermsBody`.** Legal and commercial text, so it needs legal review as well as linguistic review.
3. **D-3 Decimal separator.** The app draws numbers with a decimal point, so the copy keeps the point. Switching to a decimal comma needs a separate implementation contract (number formatting in the app), not a copy change.
4. **D-4 and D-5, fr-CH conventions.** The space before `? ! : ;`, and the 1990 reform spellings.
5. **D-7 "Fluency" rung.** The terms "Automatisieren", "Automatisation" and "Consolidamento" need confirmation.
6. **D-6 Panda Portions name, and D-8 parent register.** Both affect P1.
7. **D-9 English source wording.** "Oxford curriculum track", "Unified Math Tutor" in `footballStudioProgressOwnership`, and a missing plural in `familyStudioTutorCompletionLabel`. The Swiss copy mirrors the English, so these need a decision on the English copy.
